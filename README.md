## The entry

A one-star review of Monday, written as though Monday were a restaurant —
15 words, 90 characters, plain text, short enough to retype without trimming:

> One star. Monday's special was Tuesday, reheated, with a side of meetings I never ordered.

Why this line: the dish is the joke — Monday's own special is tomorrow,
reheated, and the side dish is meetings nobody ordered. It could not be
about any other day: only Monday serves you Tuesday before Tuesday exists.
The field is full of cold coffee, waiters who bill the whole weekend, and
diners seated by the kitchen; nobody orders the special and gets Tuesday.

## How it runs

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic: the same
single line on every run, no randomness, no network, no clock.
